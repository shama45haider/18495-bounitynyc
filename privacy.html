Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. Here's what we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it.

What We Collect

When you make a purchase, we collect your name, email address, shipping address, and payment information. Payment details are processed securely through Shopify and are never stored on our servers. We may also collect basic analytics data such as pages visited and browser type to improve the site.

How We Use It

Your information is used to process orders, send shipping updates, and respond to customer inquiries. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Cookies

This site uses cookies to improve your browsing experience. Cookies help us remember your preferences and understand how the site is used. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though some features may not work as intended.

Third-Party Services

We use Shopify for payment processing and order management. Their privacy policy applies to all transactions. We may use basic analytics tools to understand site traffic, but no personally identifiable information is shared.

Your Rights

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data. Contact us at support@bounitnyc.com and we'll take care of it.

Updates

This policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page. Continued use of the site after changes are posted means you accept the updated policy.
