Who We Are

We Are BoUnitNYC

BoUnitNYC started the same way most things in NYC do — out of pure passion. We grew up in the culture. Waiting in lines, refreshing pages at midnight, trading with people we met at the park. At some point we realized we were pretty good at finding heat, and other people wanted in. So we made it official.

Always Authentic

No fakes. No exceptions. Every piece gets looked at before it goes out the door.

New York City

Born and raised in NYC. The culture runs deep here and that's the only way we know how to do it.

Built Different

We're not a corporation. We're regular people who take this seriously and actually care about our customers.

The Real Story

We started in 2023 and haven't looked back. What began as a personal plug for friends and family turned into something we're genuinely proud of. We source Jordans, Nikes, Chrome Hearts, Amiri, Supreme — anything that matters — and we make sure every single piece is real before it gets to you.

There's a lot of people out there moving fakes and hoping you don't notice. That's not us. Our name means something to us and we're not about to put that on the line for a quick flip.

Questions?

Hit us at support@bounitnyc.com. We actually respond — no automated replies, no runaround. Just real people talking.
